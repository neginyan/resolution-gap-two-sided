\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On the periodic windows --- the family that contains paper~E's strips and the combs that undercut them --- the smallest resolution gap
is bounded from both sides with explicit assumptions and computer-assisted constants for every $10^{-100000}\le\sigma\le10^{-24}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:BF1}), and $1-\bar\varepsilon\le\liminf g_{\min}\sqrt{\ln(1/\sigma)}\le\limsup g_{\min}\sqrt{\ln(1/\sigma)}\le1$ is proved on every class with remainder bound $\bar\varepsilon\ge5.62\times10^{-7}$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:limit}), with $g\sqrt{\ln(1/\sigma)}\to1$ along the comb. The law paper~E found on the strips is thereby a theorem on a larger family on this range, and its constant is $1$ up to the class bound on paper~E's remainder; the fitted
$1.02$ reflects the slow $O(\ln\ln/\ln)$ approach. At finite blur the family's minimum is the comb, which is the strip seen through a lens
$256$ times sharper; it comes below the best Gaussian mixtures already at $\sigma\approx1.4\times10^{-3}$, not only below $7\times10^{-6}$.

The theorems are asymptotic ($\sigma\le10^{-24}$); the comb's upper bound and its remainder bound hold for every $\sigma\le10^{-24}$, and what a limit
without a class bound would still need is a bound on paper~E's remainder over a class (Lemma~\ref{lem:G2} writes $B+R_3$ exactly for every state with sufficient decay, with a state-independent Lagrange remainder, which may be a starting point); at practical resolutions paper~E's results and the numbers of Sec.~\ref{sec:finite} apply.
They are conditional on (P0), (P2)--(P7); the a-priori condition of v1.0 on $F_c$ is now a consequence (Lemma~\ref{lem:BJ1}), and the evenness of the profile is no longer needed (v1.4). For windows with at most $20$ harmonics (P5) is no longer needed
(App.~\ref{app:FN}). Windows with a bounded number of harmonics never contain the combs that approach the infimum ($|\hat a_n|\ge2-n^2(2-|\hat a_1|)$), so
the most natural next steps are a form that does not depend on the number of harmonics and general profiles at $x<1$.
For general states, cases~1 and~2 of the case split are proved, and case~3 needs one inequality (Conjecture~\ref{conj:VI}); paper~E's
Conjecture~1 is still open.
