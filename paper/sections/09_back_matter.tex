\section*{Data and code availability}
All scripts, stored results, the certificates and the verification code are available at
\url{https://github.com/neginyan/resolution-gap-two-sided} and archived at Zenodo, \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23746555}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.23746555}; a single entry point, \code{verification/run\_all.py}, checks the stored results
against their hashes and recomputes representative cells of every certificate. The computer-assisted parts of the proofs are in
\code{certificates/}: \code{bh\_cert\_cells.py} (with \code{bf\_cert\_p.py}), \code{bh\_tail.py}, \code{bf\_ay\_bootstrap.py}, \code{ba\_ivl.py},
\code{ba\_cert.py}, \code{bf\_cert\_upper.py}, \code{bg\_members.py} and \code{bh\_members\_fc.py}; for v1.1 also \code{bk\_narrow\_lemma.py} and
\code{bl\_narrow80.py} (Lemma~\ref{lem:BK1}), \code{bl\_theta.py} (App.~\ref{app:theta}), \code{bl\_chain.py} (the chain with perturbed inputs),
\code{bl\_bj1.py} (Lemma~\ref{lem:BJ1}) and \code{bm\_bl7.py} (the upper side in $\theta_\tau$ form); each reruns on a single core in under an hour.
A notebook in \code{demo/} shows the two-sided band and the comb--strip relation of Proposition~\ref{prop:BE1}.
