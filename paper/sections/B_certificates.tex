\section{The certificates}
\label{app:cert}

All computer-assisted steps verify finitely many explicit inequalities, in the sense of validated numerics~\cite{Moore1966,Tucker2011}. The programs are in the repository; each prints its constants and
writes them to a results file that the verification scripts re-read.

\paragraph*{Lower side (\code{bh\_cert\_cells.py}, with the chain of \code{bf\_cert\_p.py}).}
Cells $[\ell_a,\ell_b]$ of ratio $1.02$ cover $\ls\in[24\ln10,10^5\ln10]$ ($421$ cells). On each cell, by Lemma~\ref{lem:BH1}, the chain is evaluated
at the worst corner: $\hat\sigma=e^{-\ell_a}$ for every small factor ($10^{-300}$, an upper bound, once $\ell_a>690$), $\ell_b$ for the range of $x$ and for
$X_P$, $G=G(\ell_a)$, $\eta=1/0.9$ (all $|\varepsilon|\le0.1$ at once), and the bootstrap from $F_0=1.02G(\ell_b)$ with factor $0.9$. Each bootstrap step
evaluates (L1)--(L5): the a-priori range of $x$ (from $u_0>U_P/x$ and $L\le\sigma^{-1/2}$), $\sup x\gamma_P$, $\sup\gamma_P$, $\sup\sqrt xN_1$,
$\sup x\min(\tfrac1{16},4e^{-x})$ and the $\omega$-term. Each supremum over $x$ is bounded on a grid of cells $[x_a,x_b]$ with the $x$-factors at $x_b$
and the harmonic sums (decreasing in $x$) at $x_a$, so no maximum escapes between grid points; beyond $x=8$ all these functions decrease, and the
cells extend to at least $x_{\mathrm{low}}+20$; suprema that underflow ($x_{\mathrm{low}}\gtrsim700$) are replaced by the upper bound $10^{-300}$.
The conditions checked are: $\Phi_C$-condition, case-B threshold, $0<\beta<1$, $\nu<1$, $\omega>0$, $\varphi_1>0$, exclusion of $L>1$,
$F_{\mathrm{int}}>0$, and the sign $d\ge0$ that puts the worst point of the cell at $\ell_a$. All hold on all $421$ cells (smallest margins: case-B
threshold $>3\times10^{24}$ times $\hat\sigma$, $L>1$ exclusion by a factor $>2700$, $\beta\le0.251$, $\omega\ge0.99985$, $d\ge8.9$). $F_{\mathrm{int}}$ is taken as
$1-(\eta G\sigma\theta_G+\delta_H+r_C+r_w)$, a lower bound of the product. The roots $x_{\max}$ and $X_P$ are taken from monotone functions with a margin.
The cell value $L_{\mathrm{cell}}$ satisfies $L_{\mathrm{cell}}\ge1-1.7\ln\ell_a/\ell_a$ on every cell; the largest constant needed is $1.615$.
A numerical cross-check (not part of the proof, \code{bh\_monotone\_check.py}) re-runs the chain at interior points with the true $\sigma$, $G(\ls)$
and $|\varepsilon|\in\{0,0.05,0.1\}$: every point value is better than its cell's. The earlier point certificates (\code{bf\_cert\_p.py} at $33$ points,
\code{be\_cert\_p.py} for the version with $F_c$ and $L_w\le1$ assumed) are superseded by the cell certificate.

\paragraph*{The monotonicity table.}
Table~\ref{tab:mono} lists the constants of the chain as the certificate computes them. The two remainders of Lemma~\ref{lem:AY2P} appear after the
chain's bounds have been substituted and $W_p$ has cancelled: with $J_{vv}=1/V+J_{\mathrm{per}}$ ($W_p=\sigma^2J_{\mathrm{per}}$),
$(1-b)^2(3+VJ_{\mathrm{per}})/(8J_{vv})\le3V/8\le V/2$ and $2(L^2/V+J_{\mathrm{per}}v_*)/(8J_{vv})\le(L^2+\kappa_Qs^2/2)/4\le(L^2+s^2)/4$; then
$\sigma^4/L^4\le(\hat\sigma\psi)^2$ from (L2), $V\le V_{\max}$ from $\nu$ (where $b\le\beta W_p$ enters through $1-\beta W_{p,\max}$), $L^2\le L^2_{\max}$,
$s^2\le L^2/4$ ($\varrho\le\tfrac18$) and $(\sigma/s)^2\le\hat\sigma\Gamma_{3P}x\le\hat\sigma\Gamma_{3P}X_P$. The conditions of the certificate are, in the same
variables: $\varphi_1/\psi\ge8\psi+2\hat\sigma$ ($\Phi_C$), $\hat\sigma\le U_{\mathrm{eff}}/(32\psi(1+\hat\sigma^2)S_\gamma)$ (case B), $\beta<1$, $\nu<1$, $\omega>0$,
$\varphi_1>0$, $\varphi_1\omega^2/\Gamma_2^2>W_{p,\max}$ ($L>1$ excluded) and $F_{\mathrm{def}}<1$: each is a monotone quantity on the bad side of a constant.
A grid check moves each of $(\hat\sigma,G,\eta,\ls,F)$ with the others fixed at three base points and confirms every declared direction ($285$ checks,
\code{bi\_monotone\_table.py}; numerical, not part of the proof). It evaluates the suprema on one global $x$-grid whose first cell starts at
$x_{\mathrm{low}}$, so that a longer range only adds cells. The certificate itself re-spaces its grid over the current range; each such value is an upper
bound of the exact supremum, so validity is unaffected, but the computed values wiggle by up to $0.51\%$ along $\ls$ (\code{bi\_grid\_wiggle.py}).

\paragraph*{Tail (\code{bh\_tail.py}).}
Lemma~\ref{lem:BH2} from the last cell's constants ($K_{\mathrm{end}}$, $F_{\mathrm{int,end}}$); the explicit tail bound
$(1-F_{\mathrm{def,end}})\sqrt{\ls/(X_P+c)}/2$ with $4X_P=\ls+5\ln X_P+K_{\mathrm{end}}-\tfrac92\ln(\ls/\ell_{\mathrm{end}})$ increases from $0.99996$ to $1$, and direct
runs of the chain at $\ls=10^6,10^7,10^8$ satisfy every condition with $K_P$ below the bound of the lemma.

\paragraph*{Strips read upward (\code{bf\_ay\_bootstrap.py}).}
The strip certificate of the earlier stage, run as an upward bootstrap from $F_0\in\{0.2,\dots,0.9\}$: it closes from $F_0=0.5$ at $10^{-24}$ and
from $0.2$ at $10^{-96}$ and $10^{-1024}$, and ends at the same $F$ as the fixed-point reading. These are representative points; the continuous
statement for strips with $x\ge1$ is the cell certificate above.

\paragraph*{Upper side (\code{bf\_cert\_upper.py}; \code{ba\_cert.py} for $S'$).}
Claim: $h(k_0)=U_P\sqrt{t}-1-6.0\ln t/t<0$ on $[3.43,240]$, $t(k_0)=4k_0^2+11\ln k_0-8\ln2=\ls$ increasing, $t(3.43)\le24\ln10$,
$t(240)\ge10^5\ln10$. Numbers are carried as $\exp(\alpha k_0^2+\beta\ln k_0)\times m$ with exact rational $(\alpha,\beta)$, so the huge factors
cancel exactly; the mantissas $m$ are interval dual numbers (value and derivative enclosures) in $60$-digit decimal arithmetic, widened outward
after every operation. Harmonics: $\Qb=\bar q_1(1+E_0)$, $N_p=\bar q_1(1+E_p)$, $\sum q0_n^2=q0_1^2(1+Z_0)$, $\sum n^2q0_n^2=q0_1^2(1+Z_2)$ with
$0\le E_p\le5.8\times10^{-7}$, $0\le Z_p\le8.4\times10^{-14}$ ($a_n\le a_1$ and $K\ge10.5$ on the range); the lower sum in $\mathrm{den}_{\mathrm{lo}}$
keeps $n=1$ only. These enter as interval parameters with zero derivative, which is valid for the mean-value form at every fixed value of the
parameters. On each cell, $h\le h(m)+\max|h'|\cdot(b-a)/2$. Result: $1464$ cells, no bisection, largest upper bound $-1.35\times10^{-4}$ (for $S'$
with constant $7.4$: $1470$ cells, $-1.98\times10^{-4}$).
