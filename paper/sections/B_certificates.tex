\section{The certificates}
\label{app:cert}

All computer-assisted steps verify finitely many explicit inequalities, in the sense of validated numerics~\cite{Moore1966,Tucker2011}. The programs are in the repository; each prints its constants and
writes them to a results file that the verification scripts re-read.

\paragraph*{Lower side (\code{bh\_cert\_cells.py}, with the chain of \code{bf\_cert\_p.py}).}
Cells $[\ell_a,\ell_b]$ of ratio $1.02$ cover $\ls\in[24\ln10,10^5\ln10]$ ($421$ cells). On each cell, by Lemma~\ref{lem:BH1}, the chain is evaluated
at the worst corner: $\hat\sigma=e^{-\ell_a}$ for every small factor ($10^{-300}$, an upper bound, once $\ell_a>690$), $\ell_b$ for the range of $x$ and for
$X_P$, $G=G(\ell_a)$, $\eta=1/0.9$ (all $|\varepsilon|\le0.1$ at once), and the bootstrap from $F_0=1.02G(\ell_b)$ with factor $0.9$. Each bootstrap step
evaluates (L1)--(L5): the a-priori range of $x$ (from $u_0>U_P/x$ and $L\le\sigma^{-1/2}$), $\sup x\gamma_P$, $\sup\gamma_P$, $\sup\sqrt xN_1$,
$\sup x\min(\tfrac1{16},4e^{-x})$ and the $\omega$-term. Each supremum over $x$ is bounded on a grid of cells $[x_a,x_b]$ with the $x$-factors at $x_b$
and the harmonic sums (decreasing in $x$) at $x_a$, so no maximum escapes between grid points; beyond $x=8$ all these functions decrease, and the
cells extend to at least $x_{\mathrm{low}}+20$; suprema that underflow ($x_{\mathrm{low}}\gtrsim700$) are replaced by the upper bound $10^{-300}$.
The conditions checked are: $\Phi_C$-condition, case-B threshold, $0<\beta<1$, $\nu<1$, $\omega>0$, $\varphi_1>0$, exclusion of $L>1$,
$F_{\mathrm{int}}>0$, and the sign $d\ge0$ that puts the worst point of the cell at $\ell_a$. All hold on all $421$ cells (smallest margins: case-B
threshold $>8\times10^{23}$ times $\hat\sigma$, $L>1$ exclusion by a factor $>2700$, $\beta\le0.251$, $\omega\ge0.99985$, $d\ge8.9$). $F_{\mathrm{int}}$ is taken as
$1-(\eta G\sigma\theta_G+\delta_H+r_C+r_w)$, a lower bound of the product. The roots $x_{\max}$ and $X_P$ are taken from monotone functions with a margin.
The cell value $L_{\mathrm{cell}}$ satisfies $L_{\mathrm{cell}}\ge1-1.7\ln\ell_a/\ell_a$ on every cell; the largest constant needed is $1.615$.

\emph{Perturbed inputs (v1.1, \code{bl\_chain.py}).} The certificate is run with the $\theta_\tau$ forms of App.~\ref{app:theta} and every input lemma
perturbed by $\varepsilon=\varepsilon_{\mathrm{in}}=10^{-6}$ in the bad direction ($\epsilon_\theta<10^{-13}$ for $\tau>80$, and $\tau>80$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:BK1}):
$\eta\to\eta/(1-\varepsilon)^2$ (the $w$-information and Lemma~\ref{lem:BL4}); $U_0=F^2/(4G^2(1+\varepsilon))-\frac14$ and the conclusion divided by
$\sqrt{1+\varepsilon}$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:BL5}); in the curvature step (Lemma~\ref{lem:BL3}) the coefficient of $b$ is $(1-\varepsilon)\min(\Phi,\frac12)$ and
$\Phi\le4\min(\Phi,\frac12)$, so the case-B threshold has $128$ instead of $32$, $\beta=[\tau_0+(1+\varepsilon)\sigma^2+4r_{wp}\Phi_{b}/(1-\varepsilon)]/(\Phi_b-\tau_0)$
with $\Phi_b=(1-\varepsilon)\Phi_{\min}$, the $\omega$-term is doubled and $\omega$ carries $\sqrt{1-\varepsilon}$; $\gamma_P$, $\sigma^2$ in $(1-e-\sigma^2)$, $W_{p,\max}$,
$\Gamma_{3P}$, the $W$-factor of $L^2_{\max}$, $r_C$ and $r_w$ get factors $1+\varepsilon$ or $1+3\varepsilon$; the absolute remainder of Lemma~\ref{lem:BL3} is
relative because $\eta G\theta_G/\varphi_1\le0.68$ on every cell. All conditions hold on all $421$ cells; $L_{\mathrm{cell}}$ moves by at most $5.8\times10^{-7}$,
the largest constant needed is at most $1.6145$, and the smallest margin at $c_1=1.7$ is $5.04\times10^{-5}$ (last cell). The modifications are constants, so the
monotonicity of Lemma~\ref{lem:BH1} is unchanged. The tail (Lemma~\ref{lem:BH2}) re-run in the same form gives the bound
$(1+\varepsilon_{\mathrm{in}})^{-1/2}(1-F_{\mathrm{def,end}})\sqrt{\ls/(X_P+c)}/2$, increasing from $0.999958$; in the limit the factor
$(1+\epsilon_\theta)^{-1/2}\to1$, since $\tau\ge x_{\mathrm{low}}/(\beta W_{p,\max})\to\infty$ along the bootstrap.
A numerical cross-check (not part of the proof, \code{bh\_monotone\_check.py}) re-runs the chain at interior points with the true $\sigma$, $G(\ls)$
and $|\varepsilon|\in\{0,0.05,0.1\}$: every point value is better than its cell's. The earlier point certificates (\code{bf\_cert\_p.py} at $33$ points,
\code{be\_cert\_p.py} for the version with $F_c$ and $L_w\le1$ assumed) are superseded by the cell certificate.

\paragraph*{The monotonicity table.}
Table~\ref{tab:mono} lists the constants of the chain as the certificate computes them. The two remainders of Lemma~\ref{lem:AY2P} appear after the
chain's bounds have been substituted and $W_p$ has cancelled: with $J_{vv}=1/V+J_{\mathrm{per}}$ ($W_p=\sigma^2J_{\mathrm{per}}$),
$(1-b)^2(3+VJ_{\mathrm{per}})/(8J_{vv})\le3V/8\le V/2$ and $2(L^2/V+J_{\mathrm{per}}v_*)/(8J_{vv})\le(L^2+\kappa_Qs^2/2)/4\le(L^2+s^2)/4$; then
$\sigma^4/L^4\le(\hat\sigma\psi)^2$ from (L2), $V\le V_{\max}$ from $\nu$ (where $b\le\beta W_p$ enters through $1-\beta W_{p,\max}$), $L^2\le L^2_{\max}$,
$s^2\le L^2/4$ ($\varrho\le\tfrac18$) and $(\sigma/s)^2\le\hat\sigma\Gamma_{3P}x\le\hat\sigma\Gamma_{3P}X_P$. The conditions of the certificate are, in the same
variables: $\varphi_1/\psi\ge8\psi+2\hat\sigma$ ($\Phi_C$), $\hat\sigma\le U_{\mathrm{eff}}/(32\psi(1+\hat\sigma^2)S_\gamma)$ (case B), $\beta<1$, $\nu<1$, $\omega>0$,
$\varphi_1>0$, $\varphi_1\omega^2/\Gamma_2^2>W_{p,\max}$ ($L>1$ excluded) and $F_{\mathrm{def}}<1$: each is a monotone quantity on the bad side of a constant.
A grid check moves each of $(\hat\sigma,G,\eta,\ls,F)$ with the others fixed at three base points and confirms every declared direction ($285$ checks,
\code{bi\_monotone\_table.py}; numerical, not part of the proof). It evaluates the suprema on one global $x$-grid whose first cell starts at
$x_{\mathrm{low}}$, so that a longer range only adds cells. The certificate itself re-spaces its grid over the current range; each such value is an upper
bound of the exact supremum, so validity is unaffected, but the computed values wiggle by up to $0.51\%$ along $\ls$ (\code{bi\_grid\_wiggle.py}).

\paragraph*{Audit at interior points (v1.2, \code{bp\_audit.py}).}
Every certificate evaluates monotone factors at the ends of its cells. To find a factor whose declared monotonicity is wrong, every certificate was
re-evaluated at points \emph{inside} its cells (at least five per cell, two of them within a relative $10^{-9}$ of the ends), and every intermediate
quantity, not only the final one, was compared with the cell's value in its declared direction (relative tolerance $10^{-12}$; where the cell value
is itself a supremum over $x$ bounded on a grid, the point value is the largest value $f(x,x)$ on a grid four times finer, i.e.\ the supremum from
below, because two grids laid over two ranges give two different valid over-estimates, the wiggle noted above): the suprema over $x$ of
the chain and of Table~\ref{tab:mono}, the strip certificate, Lemma~\ref{lem:BK1} (general windows and strips at $x<1$), Lemma~\ref{lem:BJ1} (with
Table~\ref{tab:monoBJ} at random $(\sigma,c)$), the $\theta_\tau$ suprema and their monotonicity in $\tau$, the tail, $61$ cells of the lower chain with
perturbed inputs, $150$ cells of the upper side, and the class membership; in all $248$ checks, $425559$ cells and $2132407$ interior points
\numst. The only quantities on the wrong side were the factor $H_1$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:BK1} in the v1.1 code and, through it, $\kappa_s$ (and at $61$ points of the
strip cells the main factor $M$; the final ratio of each cell stayed on the right side);
with the v1.2 code there are none. The new certificates of App.~\ref{app:P5} carry the same audit ($54479$ points for Lemma~\ref{lem:E1}(ii) and
$43200$ for each $N$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:E1}(iii), none on the wrong side). The audit is numerical: it can find a wrong direction, it does not prove that
there is none; the proofs of the directions are Lemma~\ref{lem:BH1}, Table~\ref{tab:monoBJ} and the proofs in App.~\ref{app:theta}. The cells of Appendix~\ref{app:FN} carry the same audit from the start (v1.4, any phases: $17614$ interior points for Lemma~\ref{lem:F2}(i) and $67268$ for (ii), none on the wrong side).

\paragraph*{Strips read upward (\code{bf\_ay\_bootstrap.py}).}

\paragraph*{Upper side (\code{bf\_cert\_upper.py}; \code{ba\_cert.py} for $S'$).}
Claim: $h(k_0)=U_P\sqrt{t}-1-6.0\ln t/t<0$ on $[3.43,240]$, $t(k_0)=4k_0^2+11\ln k_0-8\ln2=\ls$ increasing, $t(3.43)\le24\ln10$,
$t(240)\ge10^5\ln10$. Numbers are carried as $\exp(\alpha k_0^2+\beta\ln k_0)\times m$ with exact rational $(\alpha,\beta)$, so the huge factors
cancel exactly; the mantissas $m$ are interval dual numbers (value and derivative enclosures) in $60$-digit decimal arithmetic, widened outward
after every operation. Harmonics: $\Qb=\bar q_1(1+E_0)$, $N_p=\bar q_1(1+E_p)$, $\sum q0_n^2=q0_1^2(1+Z_0)$, $\sum n^2q0_n^2=q0_1^2(1+Z_2)$ with
$0\le E_p\le5.8\times10^{-7}$, $0\le Z_p\le8.4\times10^{-14}$ ($a_n\le a_1$ and $K\ge10.5$ on the range); the lower sum in $\mathrm{den}_{\mathrm{lo}}$
keeps $n=1$ only. These enter as interval parameters with zero derivative, which is valid for the mean-value form at every fixed value of the
parameters. On each cell, $h\le h(m)+\max|h'|\cdot(b-a)/2$. Result: $1464$ cells, no bisection, largest upper bound $-1.35\times10^{-4}$ (for $S'$
with constant $7.4$: $1470$ cells, $-1.98\times10^{-4}$).

\paragraph*{Upper side beyond $k_0=240$ (v1.3, \code{bs\_upper\_tail.py}).}
Claim: $h(k_0)<0$ for every $k_0\ge240$, i.e.\ every $\sigma<10^{-100000}$ on $S'_C$. On $S'_C$ every quantity of $U_P$ is a monomial $k_0^\beta E^\gamma$, $E=e^{-k_0^2}$,
times an $O(1)$ factor, and the leading factors combine exactly. Each quantity in the formula of $U_P$ (the same lines as \code{bf\_cert\_upper.py}) is
carried as an \emph{asymptotic form}
\begin{equation*}
X=c\,k_0^{\beta}E^{\gamma}\big(1+R\,k_0^{-\nu}\big)\qquad\text{for all }k_0\ge240,
\end{equation*}
with $c$ exact in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$ (an interval only for terms that end up in a remainder), $\beta,\gamma,\nu$ exact rationals and $R$ an outward-rounded interval.
The rules: a sum keeps its leading monomial; a term with the same $\gamma$ and a smaller $\beta$ becomes the relative remainder $(c_2/c_1)k_0^{-(\beta_1-\beta_2)}$; a term with a
larger $\gamma$ is super-exponentially small and becomes $R\,k_0^{-6}$ with $R$ its value of $k_0^{\beta+6}E^{\Delta\gamma}$ at $k_0=240$ (this function decreases
for $k_0\ge240$, since $(\beta+6)/k_0<2\Delta\gamma k_0$, which is checked); products, inverses and the powers $\frac12$, $\frac32$ act on $(1+z)$, $z=Rk_0^{-\nu}$, through
the mean-value form over the range of $z$. The harmonics enter through $0\le E_p\le8E^{1.4985}$, $0\le Z_p\le8E^{2.997}$ (ratio of consecutive terms $\le\frac12$,
$K\ge0.999k_0^2$; these bounds fall with $k_0$, unlike the fixed box of the cells, which would not suffice here), $a_1=2(1+z)$ with $z\in[-1/(2k_0^4),0]$, and $\bar q_1=a_1E^{1/2}e^{w}$ with $0\le w\le32k_0^{-4}E^4$. The result is
\begin{equation*}
U_P=\frac1{2k_0}\Big(1+\frac{R_U}{k_0^2}\Big),\qquad R_U\in[3.53552,\ 3.53557],
\end{equation*}
the leading constant $\frac12$ exact ($R_U\to5/\sqrt2$: $\frac1{4k_0}$ from each branch and $\frac{\sqrt2}{k_0^3}$, $\frac1{2\sqrt2k_0^3}$, $\frac1{2k_0^5}$ at the next order).
With $\lambda=\ln k_0$: $\sqrt t\le2k_0(1+(11\lambda-8\ln2)/(8k_0^2))$, $t\le4k_0^2+11\lambda$, $\ln t\ge2\lambda+2\ln2$, so
\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
k_0^2h&\le-\Big[\tfrac{13}8\lambda+4\ln2-R_U-B\Big],\\
B&=\Big[\tfrac{11}8R_U\lambda+\tfrac{11}4(3\lambda+3\ln2)\lambda\Big]\Big/k_0^2 .
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
$B$ decreases for $k_0\ge e$ and the rest increases with $\lambda$, so the bracket is at least its value at $k_0=240$, $8.137>0$ (interval arithmetic).
The same forms give, for $k_0\ge240$, $\Qb\le10^{-12507}$, $\varrho\le10^{-50039}$ and $x\ge57600$ ((P3)--(P5)), and the $\theta_\tau$ corrections of Lemma~\ref{lem:BL7}:
$\tau$ increases with $k_0$, $1/\sigma\le\tau^2/2$ and $\sqrt t\le\tau$ give $1.5\epsilon_U+\sqrt tA_U\le(25+K_E)\tau^5\nu$, below the margin
$8.137/k_0^2$ by a factor smaller than $\exp(-10^{100078})$. Checks \numst: the forms enclose \code{bf\_cert\_upper.py}'s interval values of $U_P$ at $k_0=240$, $241.7$, $300$ and
$1000$, and every one of the $50$ intermediate forms encloses a direct $300$-digit evaluation at ten values of $k_0$ from $240$ to $10^8$ (\code{bs\_upper\_tail\_stress.py}, $1000$ comparisons). As a by-product, $\limsup_{\sigma\to0}(U_P\sqrt\ls-1)\ls/\ln\ls\le\frac{11}4$ along $S'_C$, the constant of the leading-order expansion.
